Your tmux, wherever you are.

Reattach brings the tmux you already use to iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch. No new terminal and no chat view: the same sessions and the same keys as at your desk, and a fast way to answer Claude Code and Codex permission requests from anywhere.

JUST TMUX
• Browse sessions, windows, and panes, laid out as in tmux. Tap a pane to select it
• Type directly with your own prefix and key bindings
• Helper keys on the on-screen keyboard: esc, ctrl, tab, arrows, and more. Hardware keyboard shortcuts too
• A composer for longer messages and Japanese text conversion
• tmux copy-mode selection, copied to your device clipboard when you confirm a copy
• Automatic reconnection with a visible connection status

ANSWER YOUR CODING AGENTS
• Push notifications when Claude Code or Codex needs your permission
• Every server's pending requests in one list, answered right there
• Home Screen widgets for pending requests and panes with new output
• See which panes have an agent at work

APPLE WATCH
• Read pending requests on your wrist
• Reply with a tap (Reattach Pro)
• Complications and Smart Stack widgets show how many requests are waiting

SET UP IN TWO STEPS
1. On the Mac or Linux computer that runs tmux, install reattachd and run reattachd init
2. Scan the QR code it shows
It's plain HTTPS. Use Tailscale if you already have it, or a Cloudflare Tunnel to reach your machine without opening a port. No VPN app on your iPhone, and no Reattach relay server in between. reattachd is free and open source.

THE SAME ON YOUR MAC
Reattach for Mac (free) answers the same requests from your menu bar.

REATTACH PRO (ONE-TIME PURCHASE)
• Unlimited servers (one on the free version)
• Reply from Apple Watch
• Unlimited saved commands (five on the free version)

REQUIREMENTS
• A Mac or Linux computer with tmux
• reattachd 2.1.0 or later for the Apple Watch app and reattachd init

Available in English and Japanese.

Setup guide: https://reattach.tmux.kumabook.tokyo/setup.html
