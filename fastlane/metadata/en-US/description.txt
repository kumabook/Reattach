Your terminal, on your phone.
Control your tmux sessions from iPhone and iPad, even when you are away from your computer.

TERMINAL CONTROL
• Direct input with your configured tmux prefix and key bindings
• A composer for longer messages and Japanese text conversion
• Hardware keyboard shortcuts and adjustable text size
• tmux copy-mode cursor and selection, with automatic copying to the device clipboard when you confirm a copy from the app
• Terminal links and text pasting
• Automatic reconnection with a visible connection status

YOUR WORKSPACE
• Browse sessions, windows, and panes
• Match the tmux pane layout, scroll each pane, and tap to select it
• Sync pane selection with other devices or navigate independently
• Return to your last selected pane
• Save frequent commands and reuse command history

CODING AGENTS
With optional Claude Code or Codex hooks, receive push notifications when your input is needed. Find panes waiting for permission and respond from your phone.

ENGLISH AND JAPANESE
The interface, error messages, and accessibility descriptions follow your app language.

REMOTE ACCESS
Connect using Tailscale or Cloudflare Tunnel, or use your local network.

REQUIREMENTS
• A Mac or Linux computer with tmux
• reattachd 2.0.0 or later for the new tmux and clipboard features (free and open source)

Reattach Pro unlocks additional servers, unlimited saved commands, and extended command history.

Setup guide: https://reattach.tmux.kumabook.tokyo/setup.html
