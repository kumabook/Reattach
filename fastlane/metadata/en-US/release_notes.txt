• New Apple Watch app: read pending requests on your wrist, and reply with a tap with Reattach Pro. Complications and the Smart Stack show how many are waiting.
• First launch now shows the commands to run on your computer, with a copy button. reattachd init sets up the connection, the background service, notification hooks, and the QR code in one go.
• Direct input shows esc, ctrl, tab, arrow, and symbol keys above the on-screen keyboard. With a hardware keyboard, ⌘⇧P opens permission requests and ⌘⇧I switches the input mode.
• Command history keeps 100 entries for everyone.
• Notification permission is requested after you pair a server.
• Notifications no longer show numbered reply buttons. Open a notification to answer with the full list of choices.

Update reattachd on your computer to 2.1.0 or later to use the Apple Watch app and reattachd init.
