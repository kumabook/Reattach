Reattach 2.0.0 (43)

Reattach 2.0 brings more of your tmux workflow to iPhone and iPad.

• Match the tmux pane layout, scroll each pane independently, and tap to select it.
• Use your configured tmux prefix and key bindings with direct terminal input.
• See the cursor and selection in tmux copy mode. Copies confirmed from the app automatically reach your iPhone or iPad clipboard.
• Open terminal links and paste text into the composer.
• Recover automatically from interrupted connections, with indicators for slow responses and reconnection.
• Keep pane selection in sync with other devices, or navigate independently on this device.
• Compose Japanese text, use hardware keyboard shortcuts, and adjust terminal text size.
• Find panes waiting for permission and return to your last selected pane.
• Use the app in English or Japanese, including accessibility descriptions.

Update reattachd on your computer to 2.0.0 to use the new tmux and clipboard features.
