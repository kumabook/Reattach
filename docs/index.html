Just tmux. Nothing in the way.

Reattach doesn't replace your terminal or wrap your agents. It connects to the tmux you already run.

  • Your sessions as they are

    No new shell, no chat view, no extra setup per project. Whatever runs in a pane is there.

  • Any agent, any program

    Claude Code, Codex, or anything else in tmux. Permission requests from Claude Code and Codex reach every device, and you answer them right where they appear.

  • Your keys

    Your prefix and bindings work as on your desk. Copy mode syncs with your clipboard, and Japanese input works with on-screen and hardware keyboards.

  • Your machines, connected directly

    reattachd is open source and runs on your Mac or Linux computer. Your phone connects to it over Tailscale or Cloudflare Tunnel, with no relay server in between.

Set up in two steps

Install reattachd on the computer that runs tmux. reattachd init sets up the connection, starts it in the background, adds notification hooks for Claude Code and Codex, and shows a QR code.

Run this on your computer

# macOS
brew install kumabook/reattach/reattachd
reattachd init

# Linux
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/kumabook/Reattach/main/install.sh | sh
reattachd init

Tailscale is used automatically when it's installed. For Cloudflare Tunnel, run reattachd init --url https://your-host. See the setup guide for details.

Scan the QR code

Open Reattach on your iPhone and scan the code. Your Apple Watch picks up the connection from your iPhone. On the same Mac, reattachd init connects Reattach for Mac too. For another Mac, paste the URL shown under the QR code into its settings.
